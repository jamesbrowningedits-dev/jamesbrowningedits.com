Book proposals: I help turn your idea into a proposal that gets publishers competitive. This is how I started working with Chris on Ultra-Processed People, which sold at auction.

Book partnerships: I work with you from first plan to final draft, reading as you write and helping with structure, argument and editing.

Manuscript assessment: I read your draft and write a report on what's working, what isn't, and what to do next. This can be useful before deciding what further support (if any) you need.

Developmental editing: I thoroughly edit a full draft, paying particular attention to structure, argument, pacing and voice. I send detailed notes on the manuscript, before a call to discuss it.

Line editing: I do sentence-level work to make sure the prose is clear, lively and consistent. This is best done after the structure is settled.

I work mostly on non-fiction (science, health, technology, business and ideas), but sometimes on fiction. And I work both with authors publishing traditionally and those publishing independently or as part of their business.
